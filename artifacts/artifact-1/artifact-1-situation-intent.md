# Artifact 1: Situation & Intent

## 1. Situation
The hobbits are in the Midgewater Marshes on their way to Weathertop. They lack local knowledge, environmental knowledge, and experience in assessing safe resting and foraging places. The problem is not trivial because terrain, plants, and historical danger signs are difficult to interpret, and wrong decisions directly affect health, stealth, and travel speed.

## 2. Intent
The Shire Guide is a clearly defined part of The Fellowship Companion. It supports Samwise Gamgee in making safer decisions about resting places, foraging, and immediate environmental risks in an unfamiliar environment. The application is intended to prepare incomplete information in a way that helps Sam better assess dangers and opportunities. Combat planning, long-distance communication, and overall mission coordination are explicitly not part of the application’s intent.

## 3. Value
The value of the application lies in reducing the rate of poor decisions under uncertainty. Without the tool, the group would have to make decisions about resting, gathering, and environmental risks more often without reliable points of reference, increasing the risk of delays, health problems, and detection.

## 4. System Capabilities
The system enables Sam to use the following capabilities:

1. **Classify environmental risks clearly**  
   The system should help Sam identify immediate terrain, visibility, and location-based risks and take them into account in his decisions.

2. **Support resting and camp decisions**  
   The system should help evaluate places based on safety, concealment, and suitability for a short rest.

3. **Assess plants and natural resources**  
   The system should make edible, healing, and dangerous plants or resources distinguishable so that gathering decisions become safer.

4. **Make uncertain knowledge transparent**  
   The system should indicate the reliability of hints and assessments so that Sam can distinguish rumors, historical clues, and more reliable information.

5. **Make relevant information discreetly usable under time pressure**  
   The system should help Sam quickly grasp safety-relevant information and incorporate it into his decisions even under time pressure and the need for concealment, without drawing additional attention.

## 5. Assumptions & Constraints
- The application must function offline, because no reliable connection can be expected during the journey.
- Relevant information may be incomplete or outdated; therefore, the system must make uncertainty visible rather than conceal it.
- Use must be discreet, so that the group is not more easily detected because of the application.
- The primary user has limited specialist knowledge and little time, so the application must make hints quick and easy to understand.
