# Stack 'em Gollum

A stacking game in the style of *Stack 'em Smurf*, featuring Gollum against the backdrop of Mordor.

## Gameplay

Gollums drift across the sky above Mount Doom, and pressing the **(A)** button drops them. Stack the tower high enough without letting a figure slip off, and you win — but watch out: with every successful floor, the Gollums move faster.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **(A)** | Drop the floating Gollum |
