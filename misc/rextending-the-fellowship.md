# Stack 'em Gollum
 
A stacking game in the style of *Stack 'em Smurf*, featuring Gollum against the grim backdrop of Mordor.
 
## Gameplay
 
Gollums drift across the sky above Mount Doom, and pressing the **(A)** button drops them. Stack the tower high enough without letting a figure slip off, and you win — but watch out: with every successful floor, the Gollums move faster.
 
## Controls
 
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **(A)** | Drop the floating Gollum |
 
## How Losing Works
 
There are two kinds of failure, and they behave differently:
 
- **Glancing miss** — the Gollum lands on the edge of the tower and tips off the side. You lose **one life** and play continues.
- **Complete miss** — the Gollum falls past the tower and hits the ground. **Instant Game Over**, no matter how many lives you have left.
So time your drops carefully! The lives are only a safety net for near-misses — completely missing the stack always ends the run.
